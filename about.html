About LevelUp Wallpapers

LevelUp Wallpapers is a mobile-friendly gallery of original AI-generated gaming-inspired art for phone screens. The goal is to make it simple to browse wallpapers, save favorites on your device, preview artwork, and download images.

What you can do
  • Browse a collection of portrait wallpapers.
  • Search and filter by theme.
  • Save favorites locally in your browser.
  • Download selected artwork as a PNG.
  • Add the site to your phone’s home screen for an app-like shortcut.

Original art, not official game artwork

The wallpapers are gaming-inspired digital artwork and are not intended to represent official artwork from a video game, console maker, studio, or other brand. Names and themes are descriptive. LevelUp Wallpapers is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by game publishers or console manufacturers.

Current status

LevelUp Wallpapers currently runs as a website and installable progressive web app (PWA). It is not currently listed as a native app in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Advertising is not currently active in the app.
