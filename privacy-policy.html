Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LevelUp Wallpapers (“we,” “our,” or “the site”) handles information when you visit mexiaeon.github.io/levelup-wallpapers.

Information stored on your device

The app lets you save favorite wallpapers. Your favorites are stored locally in your browser using local storage. This data stays in that browser on that device and is not sent to a LevelUp account or database because the app has no account system or app server.

The website may also use your browser’s cache and a service worker to load the app files and support app-like behavior. You can clear these items through your browser’s site-data settings.

Information handled by the hosting provider

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit a hosted website, GitHub may process technical request and security information as part of operating its service. That processing is governed by GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

At the date of this policy, LevelUp Wallpapers does not have third-party advertising, Google AdSense, Google Analytics, or advertising pixels integrated into the app. We therefore do not currently use those services through this website.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party tools are added later, this policy must be updated before those tools are enabled to explain the relevant data use, cookies, identifiers, and choices available to visitors.

Downloads

When you select “Download art,” the app creates a PNG image in your browser from the selected wallpaper and asks your browser to download it. The app does not require you to create an account to download wallpapers.

Children

LevelUp Wallpapers is a general-audience wallpaper project and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app has no account registration or user-submitted profile system.

Your choices

You can remove locally saved favorites by clearing this site’s local storage in your browser settings. You can also clear this site’s cached data using your browser’s site settings. These steps may reset favorites or app-like offline behavior on that device.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features, hosting setup, or third-party services change. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the Contact page.
